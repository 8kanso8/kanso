## 1. Campaign structure

**Goal:** Grow a Dubai audience (homeowners, landlords, investors, fellow professionals) from a Lebanon-heavy base, and turn authority into DM inquiries for the contracting company.

**Positioning:** "The contractor who tells you what other contractors won't — and shows you the work behind it."

**Audience segments:** (1) Dubai homeowners/landlords planning renovations (primary growth target). (2) Lebanese diaspora in Dubai and Lebanon-based followers with Dubai connections (bridge). (3) Construction/PM peers (credibility).

| Week | Theme | Purpose |
|------|-------|---------|
| 1 (5–11 Oct) | **Who's building this** | Establish authority and personality. Get the first Dubai-specific expertise reel in front of non-followers. |
| 2 (12–18 Oct) | **Money & mistakes** | Budget and defect content: highest save/share potential, directly useful to Dubai homeowners. |
| 3 (19–25 Oct) | **Behind the contract** | How the industry works, plus the company-launch story. Builds trust and brings in DM inquiries. |
| 4 (26 Oct–1 Nov) | **Proof & invitation** | Red flags, a transformation, a month recap. Clear invitation to DM and follow the company launch. |

**Baseline and KPIs (capture the baseline before Week 1):**
- Follower split by country: Lebanon % vs UAE % (Metricool → Instagram → country demographics).
- Weekly: reach from non-followers, shares, saves, profile visits, DMs, UAE share of new followers.
- Targets to set yourself after the baseline. I can't forecast absolute numbers for an account this new.
- Success signal: UAE share of new followers rising week over week, and Monday reels out-reaching your current average.

**Weekly rhythm:** Mon Expertise reel · Wed Behind the Scenes reel · Fri Lifestyle reel/carousel · Sun personal photo · daily Stories.

**Dubai discovery checklist for every feed post:**
- Geotag (see the Geotag column below). The Metricool post tool has no location field, so add the geotag manually when you attach media.
- 4–5 hashtags, at least 2 Dubai-specific.
- Burned-in captions, because most people watch without sound.
- Reply to every comment within the first hour.
